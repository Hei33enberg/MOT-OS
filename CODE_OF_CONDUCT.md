# Code of Conduct

## The rule

Anyone may take part in mot without harassment, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be direct. Be decent. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask before you conclude.
- A maintainer may say no, and says why.
- Credit the work of others, including work done by AI agents on someone's behalf.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexualized language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Scope

This applies in this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone speaks for
the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the lead maintainer: open a private report through the
[advisory form](https://github.com/Hei33enberg/MOT-OS/security/advisories/new) and title it `Conduct report`.
Only maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md) read these reports, and they are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants who break this
code.
