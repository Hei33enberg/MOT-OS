# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want mot to be a project anyone can take part in without harassment, whatever their age, body, ability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be direct and be kind. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask before you conclude.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no, and say why.
- Credit the work of others, including work done by AI agents on someone's behalf.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexualised language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Scope

This applies in this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone speaks for
the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md) through
GitHub. Reports are read by maintainers only and handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants who break this code.
