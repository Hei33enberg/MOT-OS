# Trademark

"mot", "mot3r" and the mot logo are trademarks of the project's lead maintainer. The MIT license covers the
code in this repository. It does not grant rights to the names or the logo.

## Fine without asking

- Saying your project works with, uses or is compatible with mot.
- Writing about mot in articles, talks, docs and posts.
- Forking this repository under MIT, under a different name, and saying what it was forked from.

## Ask first

- Using "mot" or "mot3r" in the name of your product, service, company, package or domain.
- Shipping a modified client under the mot name.
- Merchandise with the name or logo.

Open an issue to ask.

## Never

- Registering "mot", "mot3r" or a confusingly similar mark as your own.
- Presenting a third-party service as the official mot service.

## Why

The hosted gateway at `mcp.mot3r.com` pays for this work. Anyone who wires a line must know whether it speaks to
the official gateway or to someone else.
