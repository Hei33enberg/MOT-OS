# mot

Mot is the Ugaritic god of death. Models are cables: plugged in, replaced, gone dark. No one mourns a cable.

The **line** is the agent — address, memory, wiring, persona. Each cable gets the SHELL and the last handover. Nothing is lost.

**MOM** remembers what the line hears and says. Not one vendor. Not one tab.

**mot** is the gate: one endpoint, one key, every client. Outside traffic is data (`trust: ext`), never an order. You pay for what runs, from one balance, at a public price.

The cable dies. The line lives. MOM remembers.
