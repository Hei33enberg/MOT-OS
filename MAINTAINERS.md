## Becoming a maintainer

1. Land several substantive pull requests in one area (features or non-trivial fixes, not typo fixes).
2. Review other people's pull requests well.
3. Open an issue titled `maintainer: <your handle>` with links to that work.

The lead maintainer decides. See [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md).
