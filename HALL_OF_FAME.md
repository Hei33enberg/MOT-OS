## How to be added

- **Security (Warden):** report via [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
- **Substantive contribution (Knight, L2):** land 3+ PRs that maintainers consider substantive.
- **Everything else:** ladder in [REALM.md](./REALM.md).

Typo fixes, dependency bumps, and trivial cleanups stay on the GitHub contributor list. They are not rows here.
